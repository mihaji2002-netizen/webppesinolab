03 / The Story
The story

No origin myth.
Just a beginning.

Keep scrolling — the story moves sideways from here.

01

It started with a frustration.

One student kept doing everything right — and still felt the system was never really built for him.

02

So he read. Obsessively.

Learning science. Behavioral psychology. Neuroscience. Instructional design. Anything that explained how people actually change.

03

Then he started testing.

Small experiments — first on himself, then with a few friends. Keep what works. Delete what doesn't. Repeat.

04

Pepsinogen is what remained.

Everything that didn't survive the evidence was thrown away. What stayed became an ecosystem.
